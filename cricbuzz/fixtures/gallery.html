The defining moments of Ben Stokes' international career

On a Perth pitch that had cracks so wide that even a mobile phone could be inserted, Ben Stokes scored a fighting 120 for his first Test ton
 
Stokes' 85-ball century at Lord's during New Zealand's 2015 tour of England was the then fastest-ever in Tests at the venue.
 
Stokes celebrates his double century in Cape Town Test, 2016. His stunning 258 off 198 deliveries was the then fastest double-hundred in England Test history.
 
'You cannot do that Ben Stokes': His one-handed blinder to dismiss Andile Phehlukwayo headlined the 2019 World Cup opener. It was the start of a memorable summer
 
Stokes's unbeaten 84 helped England tie the 2019 World Cup final against New Zealand. England eventually clinched the trophy on boundary-count after the Super Over also ended in a tie, and Stokes was adjudged Player of the Match.
 
One of the greatest innings in English Test history, when Stokes' 135 at Headingley took the hosts to an epic 1-wicket victory over Australia in the 2019 Ashes.
 
The start of the Bazball era: Stokes walks out for toss on Day 1 of the 1st Test against New Zealand at the iconic Lord's on June 2, 2022.
 
Stokes celebrates winning the Men's T20 World Cup Final against Pakistan at the iconic MCG on November 13, 2022.
 
Stokes celebrates the wicket of Zakary Foulkes - his last scalp as an England cricketer.
 
End of an Era: Stokes gets a guard of honour as he walks out after announcing his international retirement at Trent Bridge.
 
Stokes bowed out as only the second all-rounder after Jacques Kallis with 7000+ runs and 250+ wickets in Test cricket
